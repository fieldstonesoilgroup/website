Know where your soil is going before you dig.

Fieldstone Soil Group finds, secures and coordinates receiving sites for excess soil from GTA construction and infrastructure projects.

Talk to us about a project How it works

Soil shouldn't be figured out mid-job.

On most projects, excess soil gets figured out mid-job. Sites fill up, rates change, loads get turned away and trucks sit. Every delay hits the schedule and the budget. We plan the destination first, so it doesn't.

What we do

From planning to the last load.

01

Destination planning

We match your soil's quality and volume to receiving sites before excavation starts.

02

Capacity booking

We secure space at receiving sites so your soil has somewhere to go when it's dug.

03

Move coordination

We coordinate between site, haulers and receiver so loads move on schedule with the right paperwork.

04

Pre-tender soil plans

For municipal and infrastructure owners, we set out soil destinations and costs before the project goes to tender.
